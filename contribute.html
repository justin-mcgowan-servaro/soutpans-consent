Quellerina road closure: contributions

Choose an amount and the day of the month that suits you. Your card is debited by PayFast on that day, every month, until you ask to stop.

How it works

  • You pick the day. Choose the date your salary lands and the debit follows it every month.
  • Nothing is charged today for a monthly contribution. The first debit happens on your chosen day.
  • Card payments only, handled by PayFast (used by thousands of SA businesses). We never see or store your card number.
  • Stop any time by messaging Justin. Funds are held for the road closure project and accounted for to the street.
